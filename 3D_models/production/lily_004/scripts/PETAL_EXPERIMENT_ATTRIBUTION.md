# Petal experiment attribution

The experiment adapts the length-weighted petal bend, transverse scoop, restrained tip turn, procedural surface relief, and vein detail concepts in [Digital Peony](https://github.com/LewisOrton/digital-peony), especially `src/geometry/petal-deformation.ts` and `src/rendering/petal-lookdev.ts`.

The Three.js/WebGL implementation in `petal-experiment.js` is a small, independently written adaptation for the existing six tepal islands and glTF morph targets. It does not include Digital Peony source files, WebGPU/TSL code, or XPBD simulation.
